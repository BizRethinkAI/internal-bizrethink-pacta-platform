L50: ### Definitions
L51: #### Financial Services Law (FIS) CHAPTER 18-A, ARTICLE 8
L52: § 801. Definitions. For the purposes of this article:
L53: (a) "Factoring transaction" means an accounts receivable purchase
L54: transaction that includes an agreement to purchase, transfer, or sell a
L55: legally enforceable claim for payment held by a recipient for goods the
L56: recipient has supplied or services the recipient has rendered that have
L57: been ordered but for which payment has not yet been made.
L58: (b) "Commercial financing" means open-end financing, closed-end
L59: financing, sales-based financing, factoring transaction, or other form
L60: of financing, the proceeds of which the recipient does not intend to use
L61: primarily for personal, family or household purposes. For purposes of
L62: determining whether a financing is a commercial financing, the provider
L63: may rely on any statement of intended purposes by the recipient. The
L64: statement may be a separate statement signed by the recipient; may be
L65: contained in the financing application, financing agreement, or other
L66: document signed or consented to by the recipient; or may be provided
L67: orally by the recipient so long as it is documented in the recipient's
L68: application file by the provider. Electronic signatures and consents are
L69: valid for purposes of the foregoing sentence. The provider shall not be
L70: required to ascertain that the proceeds of a commercial financing are
L71: used in accordance with the recipient's statement of intended purposes.
L72: (c) "Open-end financing" means an agreement for one or more extensions
L73: of open-end credit, secured or unsecured, the proceeds of which the
L74: recipient does not intend to use primarily for personal, family or
L75: household purposes. "Open-end financing" includes credit extended by a
L76: provider under a plan in which: (i) the provider reasonably contemplates
L77: repeated transactions; (ii) the provider may impose a finance charge
L78: from time to time on an outstanding unpaid balance; and (iii) the amount
L79: of credit that may be extended to the recipient during the term of the
L80: plan (up to any limit set by the provider) is generally made available
L81: to the extent that any outstanding balance is repaid.
L82: (d) "Closed-end financing" means a closed-end extension of credit,
L83: secured or unsecured, including equipment financing that does not meet
L84: the definition of a lease under section 2-A-103 of the uniform
L85: commercial code, the proceeds of which the recipient does not intend to
L86: use primarily for personal, family or household purposes. "Closed-end
L87: financing" includes financing with an established principal amount and
L88: duration.
L89: (e) "Finance charge" means the cost of financing as a dollar amount.
L90: It includes any charge payable directly or indirectly by the recipient
L91: and imposed directly or indirectly by the provider as an incident to or
L92: a condition of the extension of financing. It includes all charges that
L93: would be included under 12 C.F.R. part 1026.4 as if the transaction were
L94: subject to 12 C.F.R. part 1026.4. In addition, the finance charge shall
L95: include any charges as determined by the superintendent. For the
L96: purposes of an open-end financing, the finance charge shall assume the
L97: maximum amount of credit available to the recipient, in each case, is
L98: drawn and held for the duration of the term or draw period. For the
L99: purposes of a factoring transaction, the finance charge includes the
L100: discount taken on the face value of the accounts receivable.
L101: (f) "Financial institution" means any of the following: (i) a bank,
L102: trust company, or industrial loan company doing business under the
L103: authority of, or in accordance with, a license, certificate or charter
L104: issued by the United States, this state or any other state, district,
L105: territory, or commonwealth of the United States that is authorized to
L106: transact business in this state; (ii) a federally chartered savings and
L107: loan association, federal savings bank or federal credit union that is
L108: authorized to transact business in this state; or (iii) a savings and
L109: loan association, savings bank or credit union organized under the laws
L110: of this or any other state that is authorized to transact business in
L111: this state.
L112: (g) "Person" means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited
L113: liability company, joint venture, association, joint stock company,
L114: trust or unincorporated organization including, but not limited to, a
L115: sole proprietorship.
L116: (h) "Provider" means a person who extends a specific offer of
L117: commercial financing to a recipient. Unless otherwise exempt, "provider"
L118: also includes a person who solicits and presents specific offers of
L119: commercial financing on behalf of a third party. For the avoidance of
L120: doubt, the extension of a specific offer or provision of disclosures for
L121: a commercial financing, in and of itself, shall not be construed to mean
L122: that a provider is originating, making, funding or providing commercial
L123: financing.
L124: (i) "Recipient" means a person who applies for commercial financing
L125: and is made a specific offer of commercial financing by a provider. A
L126: recipient may also be an authorized representative of such person. A
L127: person acting as a broker cannot be a recipient.
L128: (j) "Sales-based financing" means a transaction that is repaid by the
L129: recipient to the provider, over time, as a percentage of sales or
L130: revenue, in which the payment amount may increase or decrease according
L131: to the volume of sales made or revenue received by the recipient.
L132: Sales-based financing also includes a true-up mechanism where the
L133: financing is repaid as a fixed payment but provides for a reconciliation
L134: process that adjusts the payment to an amount that is a percentage of
L135: sales or revenue.
L136: (k) "Specific offer" means the specific terms of commercial financing,
L137: including price or amount, that is quoted to a recipient, based on
L138: information obtained from, or about the recipient, which, if accepted by
L139: a recipient, shall be binding on the provider, as applicable, subject to
L140: any specific requirements stated in such terms.

L52: ### Sales-based financing disclosure requirements
L53: #### Financial Services Law (FIS) CHAPTER 18-A, ARTICLE 8
L54: § 803. Sales-based financing disclosure requirements. A provider
L55: subject to this article shall provide the following disclosures to a
L56: recipient at the time of extending a specific offer of sales-based
L57: financing according to formatting prescribed by the superintendent:
L58: (a) The total amount of the commercial financing, and the disbursement
L59: amount, if different from the financing amount, after any fees deducted
L60: or withheld at disbursement.
L61: (b) The finance charge.
L62: (c) The estimated annual percentage rate, using the words annual
L63: percentage rate or the abbreviation "APR", expressed as a yearly rate,
L64: inclusive of any fees and finance charges, and calculated in accordance
L65: with the federal Truth in Lending Act, Regulation Z, 12 C.F.R. §
L66: 1026.22, based on the estimated term of repayment and the projected
L67: periodic payment amounts, regardless of whether such act or such
L68: regulation would require such a calculation. The estimated term of
L69: repayment and the projected periodic payment amounts shall be calculated
L70: based on the projection of the recipient's sales, called the projected
L71: sales volume. The projected sales volume may be calculated using the
L72: historical method or the opt-in method. The provider shall provide
L73: notice to the superintendent on which method they intend to use across
L74: all instances of sales-based financing offered in calculating estimated
L75: annual percentage rate pursuant to this section.
L76: (i) The provider using the historical method shall use an average
L77: historical volume of sales or revenue by which the financing's payment
L78: amounts are based and the estimated annual percentage rate is
L79: calculated. The provider shall fix the historical time period used to
L80: calculate the average historical volume and use such period for all
L81: disclosure purposes for all sales-based financing products offered. The
L82: fixed historical time period shall either be the preceding time period
L83: from the specific offer or, alternatively, the provider may use average
L84: sales for the same number of months with the highest sales volume within
L85: the past twelve months. The fixed historical time period shall be no
L86: less than one month and not exceed twelve months.
L87: (ii) The provider using the opt-in method shall determine the
L88: estimated annual percentage rate, the estimated term, and the projected
L89: payments, using a projected sales volume that the provider elects for
L90: each disclosure, provided, that they participate in a review process
L91: prescribed by the superintendent. A provider shall, on an annual basis,
L92: report data to the superintendent of estimated annual percentage rates
L93: disclosed to the recipient and actual retrospective annual percentage
L94: rates of completed transactions. The report shall contain such
L95: information as the superintendent, by rule or regulation, may prescribe
L96: as necessary or appropriate for the purpose of making a determination of
L97: whether the deviation between the estimated annual percentage rate and
L98: actual retrospective annual percentage rates of completed transactions
L99: was reasonable. The superintendent shall establish the method of
L100: reporting and may, upon a finding that the use of projected sales volume
L101: by the provider has resulted in an unacceptable deviation between
L102: estimated and actual annual percentage rate, require the provider to use
L103: the historical method. The superintendent may consider unusual and
L104: extraordinary circumstances impacting the provider's deviation between
L105: estimated and actual annual percentage rate in the determination of such
L106: finding.
L107: (d) The total repayment amount, which is the disbursement amount plus
L108: the finance charge.
L109: (e) The estimated term is the period of time required for the periodic
L110: payments, based on the projected sales volume, to equal the total amount
L111: required to be repaid.
L112: (f) The payment amounts, based on the projected sales volume:
L113: (i) for payment amounts that are fixed, the payment amounts and
L114: frequency (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly), and, if the payment frequency
L115: is other than monthly, the amount of the average projected payments per
L116: month; or
L117: (ii) for payment amounts that are variable, a payment schedule or a
L118: description of the method used to calculate the amounts and frequency of
L119: payments, and the amount of the average projected payments per month.
L120: (g) A description of all other potential fees and charges not included
L121: in the finance charge, including, but not limited to, draw fees, late
L122: payment fees, and returned payment fees.
L123: (h) Were the recipient to elect to pay off or refinance the commercial
L124: financing prior to full repayment, the provider must disclose:
L125: (i) whether the recipient would be required to pay any finance charges
L126: other than interest accrued since their last payment. If so, disclosure
L127: of the percentage of any unpaid portion of the finance charge and
L128: maximum dollar amount the recipient could be required to pay; and
L129: (ii) whether the recipient would be required to pay any additional
L130: fees not already included in the finance charge.
L131: (i) A description of collateral requirements or security interests, if
L132: any.

L50: ### Disclosure requirements for renewal financing
L51: #### Financial Services Law (FIS) CHAPTER 18-A, ARTICLE 8
L52: § 808. Disclosure requirements for renewal financing. If, as a
L53: condition of obtaining the commercial financing, the provider requires
L54: the recipient to pay off the balance of an existing commercial financing
L55: from the same provider, the provider must disclose:
L56: (a) The amount of the new commercial financing that is used to pay off
L57: the portion of the existing commercial financing that consists of
L58: prepayment charges required to be paid and any unpaid interest expense
L59: that was not forgiven at the time of renewal. For financing for which
L60: the total repayment amount is calculated as a fixed amount, the
L61: prepayment charge is equal to the original finance charge multiplied by
L62: the amount of the renewal used to pay off existing financing as a
L63: percentage of the total repayment amount, minus any portion of the total
L64: repayment amount forgiven by the provider at the time of prepayment. If
L65: the amount is more than zero, such amount shall be the answer to the
L66: following question:
L67: "Does the renewal financing include any amount that is used to pay
L68: unpaid finance charge or fees, also known as double dipping? Yes, {enter
L69: amount}. If the amount is zero, the answer would be No."
L70: (b) If the disbursement amount will be reduced to pay down any unpaid
L71: portion of the outstanding balance, the actual dollar amount by which
L72: such disbursement amount will be reduced.
