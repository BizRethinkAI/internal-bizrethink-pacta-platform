# Section-by-section register

Every section or subsection block listed below was read end to end. The finding
column is analysis of where the duty operates, **not a transcription** and not
an assignment of ADR metadata. Read each entry subject to the scope/exemptions
and [currency limits](README.md) for that state. “No additional agreement text”
means none required **by that section**; it does not mean the agreement is free
of duties under other law.

Kinds: **scope** (definitions/exemptions); **agreement** (required contractual
content or conditional writing); **prescribed** (specified wording/form);
**disclosure** (information delivered to recipient); **conduct** (operational
compliance); **restriction** (prohibited/limited term or practice); **enforcement**.

## Clause-specific authority still outside this walk

The section walk does not absorb all cross-referenced law. In particular,
statutory definitions using Article 9 concepts do not establish attachment,
perfection or priority for an actual transaction; Regulation Z imports for a
commercial disclosure do not classify that transaction as consumer credit;
electronic-signature permission does not prove the signing workflow satisfies
E-SIGN/UETA. Full-performance guaranties, reconciliation/loan characterization,
bankruptcy treatment, arbitration enforceability and ACH operating rules need
the relevant primary authority when those clauses are addressed.

These are targeted clause dependencies, not reasons to restart the dedicated
eleven-state source walk or label all unexplained clauses `discretionary`.
