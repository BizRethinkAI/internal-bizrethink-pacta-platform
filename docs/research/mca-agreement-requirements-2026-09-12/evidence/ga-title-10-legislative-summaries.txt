Act 605; SB 73
This Act revises provisions concerning advertisements for legal services, including provisions
concerning telephone solicitations and authorizes the State Bar of Georgia to promulgate rules and
regulations that require certain disclosures regarding advertisements for legal services, and provides
for penalties for violations of such rules and regulations. The Act further provides that injunctive
relief and damages may be sought for certain violations of such rules and regulations regarding
unwanted telephone solicitations.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-1-424.1 and 46-5-27.
Effective May 6, 2024.
Act 403; HB 1018
This Act establishes the usage of a code by a financial institution which denotes a transaction as
one with a firearms retailer as an illegal unlawful discriminatory practice. The Act prohibits
financial institutions from declining lawful payment card transactions based upon the sole fact that
the transaction was with a firearms retailer. The Act amends the procedure for obtaining a remedy
against a financial institution alleged to have engaged in discriminatory practices relating to firearms
retailers by providing notice and right to cure. The Act prohibits the establishment of a
multijurisdictional data base by a government entity for firearm ownership and applications for
weapons carry licenses.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-1-439.1 through 10-1-439.3 and 16-11-129.
Effective July 1, 2024.
Act 564; SB 472
This Act enacts the "Combating Organized Retail Crime Act," which revises the definitions of
"high-volume third-party seller," "seller," and "third-party seller."
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Section 10-1-940.
Effective July 1, 2024.
Act 577; HB 934
This Act allows for enforcement of unsigned rental agreements under certain circumstances,
resulting in the vacating and removal of personal property from self-storage facilities. The Act
further allows for the execution and delivery of rental agreements electronically.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Section 10-4-213 and enacts O.C.G.A. Section 10-4-218.
Effective July 1, 2024.

Act 278; SB 40
This Act broadens the legal applicability of the term "used, detached catalytic converter" and
removes an exception applicable to used, detached catalytic converters. The Act prohibits secondary
metals recyclers from paying to any seller more than $100.00 in cash for any transaction or
completing more than two transactions per seller, per day, per registered recycler location; or from
paying cash to any seller for used, detached catalytic converters or coils, used utility wire, used
communications copper, copper wire, or battery. The Act provides for the use of certain registration
fees and provides for records and reporting. The Act permits certain caps for secondary metals
recyclers relative to payments to and the number of transactions with sellers to remain in effect after
January 1, 2026. The Act further revises provisions relating to the $1.00 surcharge on certain waste.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-1-350, 10-1-351, 10-1-355, 10-1-358, 10-1-359.1,
10-1-359.2, 10-1-359.3, 10-1-359.5, and 12-8-39.
Effective July 1, 2025, and shall apply to all transactions occurring on and after such date.
Act 300; SB 213
This Act makes it a violation of the Fair Business Practices Act of 1975 for any person to provide
scholastic cheating services to any student or examinee. The Act provides that only the Attorney
General may enforce violations of the Act and that the Act shall not be interpreted to prohibit
tutorial services or certain other academically supportive services.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-1-393 and 10-1-399 and enacts O.C.G.A. Section
10-1-393.20.
Effective July 1, 2025.
Act 177; HB 240
This Act provides that, under certain circumstances, the use of mortgage trigger leads in consumer
transactions shall be considered an unfair or deceptive act or practice and provides for penalties.
The Act also provides for definitions.

Act 282; SB 81
This Act provides that written lists of consumer data obtained from motor vehicles dealers and all
persons to whom any consumer data has been provided by franchisors, manufacturers, distributors,
or affiliates thereof shall not be required to include a person to whom consumer data was provided,
or the specific consumer data provided to such person, if it would require a franchisor, manufacturer,
distributor, or affiliate thereof to violate any applicable restrictions on reuse or disclosure of the
consumer data established by law. The Act provides for limitations, procedures, and conditions on
the use of motor vehicle dealer data and for standards regarding access and integration of data
collected and possessed by dealers and third parties. The Act creates various unlawful activities by
franchisors and delineates specific activities which qualify as activity as a new motor vehicle dealer.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-1-621, 10-1-632, 10-1-641, 10-1-662, and 40-2-39 and
enacts O.C.G.A. Section 10-1-633.
Effective July 1, 2025.
Act 285; SB 112
This Act provides that any manufacturer's warranty of an HVAC system installed in residential
property that is in effect at the time such property is sold shall automatically transfer to the new
owner of such property and shall continue in effect as if the new owner of such property was the
original purchaser of the HVAC system. The Act further provides that any manufacturer's,
distributor's, or retailer's warranty of an HVAC system shall be effective on the date such system
is installed by a licensed contractor and shall not be conditioned upon the registration of such
system.
The Act enacts Article 37 of O.C.G.A. Chapter 10-1.
Effective January 1, 2026, and applicable to any warranty of an HVAC system or component
thereof that is manufactured and issued, sold, or offered for sale by a manufacturer, distributor, or
retailer on or after such date and to any modifications or extensions of any such warranty issued,
sold, or offered for sale on or after such date.
Act 125; HB 131
This Act revises the advertisement requirements applicable to nonjudicial enforcement of an
owner's lien upon personal property located within a self-service storage facility.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Section 10-4-213.
Effective July 1, 2025, and applicable to any actions for lien enforcement without judicial
intervention initiated on or after such date.

Act 377; SB 284
This Act authorizes the Secretary of State to issue orders requiring violators of certain securities
laws to repay investors, customers, or clients sums paid in connection with securities purchases.
The Act provides for the award of low-wealth capital outlay grants to qualifying local school
systems that award certain contracts for roofing improvements to suppliers that meet certain
criteria. The Act further provides that certain contracts for roofing improvements be deemed to
have complied with notice and competitive award requirements for certain public works
construction contracts.
The Act amends O.C.G.A. Sections 10-5-73, 20-2-262, 36-91-2, and 36-91-21.
Effective April 22, 2026, and applicable to all contracts entered into on or after such date.
Act 445; HB 1112
This Act provides for methods of rounding procedures for purchases when using legal tender
following the discontinuation of the penny. The Act requires that where offered, a merchant must
accept exact change before any rounding. The Act further requires that taxes on such transactions
be computed before rounding.
The Act enacts Article 38 of O.C.G.A. Chapter 10-1.
Effective July 1, 2026.
Act 510; SB 439
This Act requires referral agencies making referrals to assisted living communities or personal
care homes to conspicuously disclose certain information to a prospective resident or their
authorized representative. The Act requires such referral agencies to obtain acknowledgement of
receipt of such disclosed information from a prospective resident or their authorized representative.
The Act prohibits referral agencies from charging or collecting a fee from an assisted living
community or personal care home unless certain conditions are met. The Act further provides for
the enforcement of these requirements by the Attorney General.
The Act enacts O.C.G.A. Section 10-1-393.22.
Effective July 1, 2026.
